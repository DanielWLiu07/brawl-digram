Pick a brawler + variant, click "place", then click the map. Select a token and move the mouse to aim. Direct-fire reticles (cone/line/dash) are clipped by walls; throwers (Dynamike, Barley…) land wherever you point, clamped to their max-range ring. Movement range is that brawler's speed × the time window, flood-filled around walls. Drag a token to reposition.

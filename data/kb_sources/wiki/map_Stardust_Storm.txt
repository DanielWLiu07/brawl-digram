==Tips==
*Sharpshooters and medium-ranged Brawlers can snipe enemies on the sides.
*Tanks can use the bushes to ambush enemies. However, they are not recommended because players at high ranks are alert and also check bushes for hiding enemies.
*Most people will use anti-tanks on this map, so having an Assassin like [[Mortis]], [[Edgar]], [[Crow]], [[Leon]], or [[Buzz]] on your team may be a good choice.
*Support Brawlers should move upwards in the left lane to take down incoming enemies.
*Throwers can use the walls just below or beside the mine to control the middle. However, beware of Brawlers with wall-breaking abilities.
*[[Pam]] can place her healing turret behind the wall just below the mine. When your teammates are at low health, they can come over to their, heal up, and continue the fight.
*[[Penny]]'s mortar or [[Mr. P]]'s porter spawner can be placed behind the walls just below the mine to constantly damage and/or annoy the enemies.

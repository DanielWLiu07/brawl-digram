==Tips==
*Sharpshooters such as [[Piper]], [[Brock]], [[Nani]], and [[Colt]] are extremely good on this map. By destroying the few 4x1 walls clusters near the enemy's spawn with their Super, they can force enemies out of hiding if they choose to move forward. Their high damage output and extremely long range gives them an enormous advantage over close-ranged Brawlers.
**[[Brock]] with his Rocket Fuel Gadget can pressure enemies from dodging shots by forcing them to take damage if they choose to fall back. He, too, can destroy the 4x1 wall cluster (better than Piper since he can destroy them at a moderately far range) to force enemies to hide closer to the middle.
*With the walls still intact, throwers such as [[Tick]] and [[Sprout]] can reliably push back enemy Brawlers and limit enemy movements by hiding behind the 4x1 walls and blocking off chokepoints. However, [[Barley]] and [[Dynamike]] are mediocre alternatives due to their small area control. For Tick, it's preferred to use either Gadget (Mine Mania if you are offensive; Last Hurrah if you are better up relatively close) and his Well Oiled Star Power and for Sprout, its Garden Mulcher Gadget and its Photosynthesis Star Power.
*Medium-ranged Brawlers should be able to supply large amounts of support if they would like to succeed with the long-ranged meta of this map. [[Bo]]'s Super allows him to stop enemy progress, or otherwise deal considerably amounts of damage. The sparse amount of bushes on the map make his Circling Eagle Star Power a less than ideal choice; Snare a Bear not only makes his Super stun enemies, making them easy targets to pick off, but enemies will have to go around them or race across them if they want to defuse the mines.
*[[Penny]]'s can act as a decent replacement to throwers. Her turret leaving behind an area of fire allows for great area control and high damage, making it ideal for controlling and taking down the enemy team. Penny's above-average range also allows her to function as a decent sharpshooter, although she cannot act as a sniper replacement because of her moderate damage output.
*Close-ranged Brawlers such as [[Bull]], [[Rosa]], [[Edgar]], [[El Primo]], [[Darryl]], [[Sam]], [[Lily]], [[Doug]], [[Jacky]], [[Hank]], [[Mortis]], [[Bibi]], or [[Shelly]] are not viable in this map because they will be out-ranged by sharpshooters and throwers without ever posing a significant threat to the enemy team. If playing a close-ranged Brawler is unavoidable, always try and push the enemies back.
*[[Nani]]'s long range and high damage allows her to snipe enemies before they can attack her. Staying near your spawn and behind walls when using your Super also allows you to deal high damage (especially with her Autofocus Star Power) to heavily damage or even knock out enemies.
*[[Byron]] is a good support Brawler, especially with his long attack range, allowing him to heal allies at a long range. Both of his Star Powers are good: Injection allows Byron to heal his two other teammates simultaneously and/or damage enemies while doing so, while Malaise effectively increases the chances of an enemy elimination by one of your teammates. Stay are closer to your spawn since Byron has low health and instead use his long attack range to heal, only going closer to heal teammates with Byron's Super.

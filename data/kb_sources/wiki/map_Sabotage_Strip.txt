==Tips==
*Long-ranged Brawlers like [[Amber]], [[Belle]], [[Colt]], [[Brock]], [[Bea]], [[Piper]], [[8-Bit]], [[Byron]], and [[Rico]] are great here because the map is very open, and they can shoot across both zones when they're in the middle.
*Despite the amount of bushes, tanks are not viable as unlike sharpshooters they do not have enough range to make use of the middle lane.
*[[Buzz]], [[Darryl]], and [[Carl]] have abilities that allow them to bypass the rope fences. Buzz and Darryl have their Super and Carl has his Flying Hook Gadget.
*Before destroying the fences check to see if the enemy team has a heavyweight or assassin. If they do, do not destroy them.

==Tips==
*[[Jessie]] and [[Penny]] can use the yellow arcade blocks to protect their turrets. Jessie should place hers in front of it while Penny should place her turret inside of it.
*The bush strips are far away from the center and have little impact on gameplay. Except for heading there as a heavyweight, move diagonally towards the 2x2 bush and then the enemy's yellow arcade block.
*Long-ranged Brawlers have more room for open engagement, but they should play as support because most of the walls are located near the center. They should play near the right lane where enemies cannot use the bush strips to sneak up on them easily.
*Area denial Brawlers work very well on this map. [[Emz]], Penny, and [[Amber]] can block off two major entrances using their main attack and catch Brawlers behind walls using their Super.
**[[Poco]], [[Sandy]], [[Frank]], and [[Pam]] can stay within the zone for long periods of time, although they do not have the burst damage that Emz, Penny, and Amber are capable of.
*[[Rosa]] can enlarge the 2x2 bush in the left lane using her Grow Light Gadget to help retain her cover. They can also connect the left bush strip if they wish to be an aggressive ambush Brawler.
*Wide spread Brawlers such as Pam, Emz, [[Tara]], [[Gene]], Penny, [[Gale]], and Poco can keep enemies back by consistently firing beyond the zone.
*Throwers, such as [[Barley]], [[Sprout]], [[Tick]], [[Dynamike]], [[Grom]], and [[Larry & Lawrie]], are recommended, as they can stand behind the walls and continuously fire attacks into the zone, providing area denial.
*Brawlers that counter area-denial Brawlers such as [[Edgar]], [[Mico]], [[Buzz]], [[Kenji]], or [[Alli]] can be good picks here also, as area-denial Brawlers are very popular picks here.

==Tips==
*[[Bull]] is an ideal Brawler for this map, given that his Super can destroy obstacles. Once you have your Super, aim straight for the middle of the two barriers that block the goal. This will make it much easier for your teammates to score a goal.
*[[Shelly]], [[Colt]], [[Frank]], [[Pearl]], and [[Brock]] are also decent Brawlers for this map because they can destroy walls and barrels to clear the opposing obstacles, clearing the way for their teammates.
*[[Barley]], [[Dynamike]], [[Grom]], Sprout, [[Tick]], and [[Larry & Lawrie]] are useful for defeating opponents behind the main wall.
*[[Sprout]] can use its Super to block the fastest route to the goal for the enemy team, making the enemy team take the long route. This allows your team get the ball faster than the enemy.
*[[Darryl]] can use his Super after shooting the ball forward to get to the goal. The side lanes help Darryl with this and since the barriers aren't in his way. He can easily surpass opponents.
*[[Spike]] is a great Brawler to search the left and right lanes of the grass for any enemies trying to sneak up on him, as well as his gadget Popping Pincushion can identify where an enemy is in the grass.
*The abundance of bushes is great for [[Rosa]]. Connect the bottom bushes to the main area of bushes with your Grow Light Gadget for maximum stealth with Rosa. One can also use her Unfriendly Bushes Gadget and walk up to any brawlers that when slowed are vulnerable, such as most sharpshooters or throwers.
*[[Squeak]] can block off the main entrance points with his attacks, allowing your team to get to the ball first while the enemy team tries to avoid his attacks.
*If [[El Primo]] has his Super, he can shoot the ball right in front of the enemy's center barriers, then use his Super on the ball to break open the barriers and land on the ball. Once the center barriers are broken, El Primo is standing in front of a wide open goal and can just shoot the ball in or walk it in.
*[[Rico]] is a decent Brawler in this map as his main attack and Super are able to bounce off the walls and counter tanks like Frank, Rosa, Darryl, Bull, and El Primo.

==Tips==
*If you stand right behind the center bottom wall as [[Crow]], you can just barely jump over the first wall and attack the safe. A few other Brawlers with aerial Supers can skip the bush cluster entirely.
*Throwers can support teammates heading into the bush cluster, after they do. They should move behind the wall in the left lane.
*Kaboom Canyon has lots of bushes in the center of the map. This is great when you want to get to the other side without being detected. It is recommended to use high burst damage Brawlers, or medium-ranged Brawlers with high utility.
*As short-ranged Brawlers get closer to the safe their value decreases. To maximize their survival time, they should stay within the bushes as falling back will leave them trapped.
*The right lane lacks walls that Brawlers will have to rely on, this lane is most suitable for long-ranged Brawlers that can use it to target incoming heavyweights.
*Use [[Colt]]'s Silver Bullet Gadget or [[Brock]]'s Rocket Fuel Gadget to open the walls and bushes in the middle, but try not to open the ones protecting your safe.
*[[Bibi]] with her Home Run Star Power can outrun any Brawler and hide in the bushes and surprise any enemies that enter the bush cluster. Her Vitamin Booster gadget also helps her fighting sharpshooters and other medium-ranged brawlers.
*Throwers such as [[Tick]], [[Dynamike]], [[Grom]], [[Larry & Lawrie]], and [[Barley]] can stand behind the wall in between the ponds, and deal lots of damage to the safe.
*Throwers can also juke around the right bottom side 9x9 wall to deal damage while avoiding enemy fire.
*Brawlers such as [[Bull]], [[El Primo]], [[Darryl]], [[Leon]], [[Rosa]], and [[Shelly]] can hide in the middle of bushes to defend and attack enemies from approaching the Heist.
*[[Colette]] can be useful because she can counter tanks and since there is so much space you can attack brawlers from a long range and use the super to either kill enemies if defending, or against the safe if attacking. She is very good because it only takes a few hits to charge super.
*[[Belle]] is good at defending the enemy team her Super makes it easier to defeat mid to high health Brawlers like [[Darryl]], [[Carl]], [[8-Bit]], [[Bibi]], [[Bonnie]], [[R-T]], [[Pam]], [[Doug]], and [[Buzz]] she can also attack behind the enemy wall from a distance while defending her Nest Egg Gadget is great at slowing aggressive Brawlers such as [[Shelly]], [[Emz]], [[Fang]], [[Darryl]], [[El Primo]], [[Bull]], and [[Max]].
*[[Eve]] is great at defending, thanks to her Trait that allows her to walk over water, giving her a huge advantage on this map she can easily swarm it with her hatchlings, whether it's her Gotta Go! Gadget, her Happy Surprise Star Power, or her Quadruplets Mythic Gear which are good at defending and attacking the safe or the opponents.
*[[Brock]] is amazing at melting the safe, whether he is using his More Rockets Star Power or his Rocket No. Four Star Power, it's recommended to use his Rocket Fuel Gadget as it can break the wall of your side thus making you and your teammates hitting your shots easier against the enemy team and due to his long range he can attack behind the enemy wall giving him a defensive advantage.
*[[Bonnie]] is versatile while in her Clyde form, as she can snipe other Marksmen like [[Piper]], [[Mandy]], [[Belle]], [[Brock]], [[Nani]], and [[Bea]], and her Super can be used to eliminate other Brawlers, or jump on the safe dealing high damage.

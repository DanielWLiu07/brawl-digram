==Tips==
*Since the walls and bushes form many potential ambushing spots, high-health close-range Brawlers, such as [[El Primo]], [[Bull]], [[Darryl]], [[Sam]], [[Draco]], [[Ash]], Frank, and [[Rosa]], are recommended.
*Destroying the walls of the other side allow for easier shots and approaches, making wall-breaking Brawlers a possible choice.
*The diagonal bushes are dangerous locations for sharpshooters that have a hard time checking them. [[Gene]], [[Poco]], [[Spike]], [[Surge]], Frank, [[Pam]], and [[Gale]]'s wide spread attacks makes them an ideal teammate.
*Entering the side entrances to the goal are great options if your teammate can push back the opponents' lanes. However, teammates often stay in the same lane to help each other.
*If Brawlers can't guarantee a goal, they should wait behind the center wall clusters until their teammates help clean up the enemy team.
*Destroying the walls in front of the goal post is beneficial as there is no direct barrier blocking the goal.
*Because of the significant amount of walls, throwers are great on this map. On the other hand, sharpshooters are discouraged (with the rare exception of [[Rico]]).
*[[Mr. P]], [[Squeak]], [[Berry]], [[Willow]], or throwers can shoot over the walls in the middle, making enemies hiding behind them retreat.
*A common strategy on this map is using [[Frank]] with his Sponge Star Power and rushing the goal with the ball; Frank's health is often enough to tank the damage and advance through the opponents' fire.
*Due to the abundance of walls, throwing the ball into a difficult-to-access region can be helpful in defending.

==Tips==
*Teams should spread out for more control as the map has less walls than others. Ideally, two Brawlers should push towards the enemy team's zone while a teammate stays back to control the zone closest to the player's team.
*Throwers should stick to the 4x1/5x1 fences as most enemies will move through the side bushes.
*Brawlers heading towards the zone furthest from the player's team should have an above-average movement speed to assert constant pressure on the enemy team.
**[[Max]], [[Carl]], [[Stu]], and [[Leon]] with their Supers and [[Bibi]] with her Home Run Star Power can dodge shots using the yellow arcade blocks and they can use their tremendous speed to buy time for their teammates to head in with them, while Max can use her Super for teammates that are tagging along.
*[[Pam]] and [[8-Bit]]'s health, damage, and range make them great defenders for the left zone. Even if enemies dominate them frequently, they will be able to do considerable poke damage until their teammates can help them regain control.
*Brawlers heading through the center lane need to have medium to long range to compensate for the lack of bushes.
*[[Bull]] and [[El Primo]] can be used as they both are heavyweights and their Supers can be used to go to the other zone and deal with the enemy team.
*Throwers such as [[Tick]], [[Dynamike]], [[Larry & Lawrie]], [[Grom]], and [[Barley]] are good picks on this map, as they can hide behind the walls in each zone to evade enemy fire, while still dealing damage. Played offensively, as they can easily keep the enemy team trapped in their spawn point, as there are only two small choke points out.
*[[Jessie]] is an amazing last-pick option in this map because her turret can sweep and check the bushes well. Moreover, this is even better if the enemy team's offensive Brawlers have a slow reload speed, making her turret a menace to them. When paired with Jessie's Energize Star Power, it can be almost impossible for the enemy Brawler to take it out alone.
*[[Amber]] is great for control in this map, she can use her Super to burn the bushes which allows the enemy team to access the zone on your side. She can also use her Super to trap the enemies at their spawn or to discourage the enemies from entering your team's zone.
*[[Eve]], [[Juju]], and [[Angelo]] are good for this map, as they can traverse the lakes using their special Traits for crossing water, leading to a safe place to attack opponents.
*[[Doug]] is a great last pick for this map, as his Gadget allows him to pick up kills easily. He can also use his Super to gain more zone time.

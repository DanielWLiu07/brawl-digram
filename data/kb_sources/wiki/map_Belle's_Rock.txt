==Tips==
*This map is useful for throwers because there is lots of cover which can cover them from attacks. [[Sprout]] can also use its Super to block enemy paths, which can help your team defend if you have more members of your team left or are ahead in a tiebreaker. Paired with its Transplant Gadget, Sprout can keep repeatedly blocking off parts of the map. Other throwers like [[Dynamike]], Barley, [[Larry & Lawrie]], and [[Tick]] are good on this map to pressure the opposing team. They can stand behind the L-shaped walls and provide constant pressure.
*[[Mr. P]]'s attack bounce mechanic and Super makes him a good Brawler on this map. His Handle with Care Star Power makes his attack's bounce more deadly and forces enemies to move towards Mr. P. 
*To counter throwers, destroy the center walls. [[Brock]]'s Rocket Fuel Gadget and Super, [[Colt]]'s Silver Bullet Gadget and Super, and [[Piper]] and [[Nani]]'s Supers are good wall-breaking abilities. Destroying walls also gives these sharpshooters an advantage, as there is less hindrance due to less walls on the battlefield for throwers to hide behind.
*[[Rico]] and [[Ruffs]] are decent Brawlers in this map as they can use the walls to bounce their attacks and safely damage enemy Brawlers.
*[[Grom]] can possibly be good on this map due to the center of the map fitting the way his attack splits.
*Throwers can stand in the middle area, protected by the walls and being open enough for them to move around and if needed to heal or support like [[Barley]] with his Herbal Tonic Gadget. They can escape through either side if necessary.
**Throwers can attack behind the L-shaped walls. If an opponent notices you, you can also easily retreat.
**Among sharpshooters, [[Brock]] and [[Rico]] are best first-picks for the map. [[Brock]]'s explosion radius of his main attack often makes it easier to hit the enemies and is very useful at controlling the narrow pathways in the map. Both his gadget and super can be used to break the walls and create a comfortable damage output environment for him and his teammates. [[Rico]] can use his bouncing ability to damage enemies while ensuring his own safety. [[Piper]] is also a possible choice, but it would be better for her if the middle walls are broken.

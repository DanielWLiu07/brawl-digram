==Tips==

* Short-range Brawlers are risky to play on this map as wall breakers, such as [[Colt]], [[Shelly]], [[Frank]], [[El Primo]], [[Bull]], [[Grom]], [[Piper]], [[Tick]], [[Nani]], [[Pearl]], [[Dynamike]], or [[Brock]], can open up the map, making it harder to approach the enemy.

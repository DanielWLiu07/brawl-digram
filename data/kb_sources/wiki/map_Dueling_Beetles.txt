==Tips==
*Long-ranged Brawlers have little to no room to use their extra range. Instead, use medium-ranged Brawlers that still act as ranged counters to heavyweights.
**[[Bo]], [[Squeak]], [[Grom]], [[Spike]], [[Griff]], and [[Tara]] have Supers with excellent area control that can handle enemy teammates that group up. These Brawlers should capture zones otherwise because of their considerable bulk.
*[[Rosa]]'s Plant Life Star Power in conjunction with her Grow Light Gadget allows her to sustain ambushes for longer periods of time if walls stay up.
*Throwers can block off lanes entirely ensuring damage to unaware opponent Brawlers. [[Tick]] and [[Barley]] have the best area control due to the mechanics of their attacks, making them more dangerous and powerful on this map than other throwers.
**Alternatively, [[Sprout]] is a great choice due to its Super that can stack and block enemies away by using the Super in the opening between the bushes nearest to the Hot Zone.
*A good strategy is to place [[Gale]]'s Spring Ejector Gadget directly behind the 5 tile long wall which is the closest obstacle to the spawn areas. This will help him and his teammates a quicker and more direct route to the center of the map.
**Alternatively, [[Gray]] can create portals using his Super at this location to get the same effect of transporting allies quicker.
*If you're not comfortable using a thrower on this map, you can always use Brawlers like [[Jacky]], [[Penny]], [[Hank]], [[Emz]], [[Poco]], [[Doug]], [[Sandy]], [[Mr. P]], or [[Frank]], since they offer a lot of area control. They are also relatively easy to use, so they make great choices for players who struggle.
*Penny's Super is very effective. Place the turret inside the bend at the sides of the map for incredible value. The turret's fireballs will deny a large area of the map while non-Thrower enemies will struggle to destroy it. 
*Control the center as much as you can by eliminating your opponents and/or healing your team. This allows you and your team to easily win the game. Also, there is only one hot zone which means you have no second chances of regaining the game once control is lost. 
**Do not let the opponents enter the center as it is hard to regain control once the opponent gets a hold on the Hot Zone.
*Try to remove the bush on the enemy's side to avoid any surprise attack by a heavyweight or harassment by a thrower. If not done so, you're also more vulnerable to losing control of the valuable center.
*[[Amber]] and her Wild Flames Star Power allows her to block both of the enemy team's choke points. Also with her Fire Starters Gadget she can get additional charge for her Super during battle.
*[[Sandy]] is a strong Brawler on this map, because his Super has a huge area. His attack is also good for getting rid of clumped-up enemies.
*[[Poco]] is a strong support Brawler on this map. Since many of your teammates and enemies will be bunched up near the center, it will be easy to hit them with your attack or Super. His Screeching Solo Star Power allows Poco to finish off low-health enemies or to sweep the bush near the enemy team's spawn.
*[[Doug]] is a supremely strong Brawler on this map, because the bushes near the zone offer cover for him while he can heal his teammates. Similar to [[Jacky]], he can deal damage from behind a wall, negating the cover of the opponent's walls near the zone.

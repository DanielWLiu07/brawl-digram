==Tips==
*[[Bull]], [[Frank]], and [[El Primo]] are ideal Brawlers for this map, given their Supers that can destroy obstacles. Once your Super is charged up, aim straight for the three barrels that protect the goal from direct shots. This will make it much easier for your teammates to score a goal.
*[[Rico]] can either use the straight lane vertical wall to attack Brawlers in front of the L-shaped wall or use the zig-zag lane to attack Brawlers hiding behind the 4x4 ones. He can also use his Multiball Launcher Gadget to further scout the map.
*[[Bibi]] can use the small three tile walls to attack Brawlers around them without taking damage. The straight lane vertical wall is very suitable for a Super bubble which can bounce off these walls with ease.
*The general nature of this map leads to Brawlers that are clumped up which leaves easy team-wiping opportunities that [[Tara]], [[Mr. P]], [[Emz]], [[Squeak]], [[Amber]], [[Penny]], and [[Carl]] can take hold of.
*[[Jacky]] can make use of the thin walls particularly the straight lane vertical wall which is often used by tanks and marksmen for protection.
*The left lane is best used by marksmen, center by tanks, and right lane by fighters. Taking control of all three lanes is crucial because of the 4x4 walls which punish slower Brawlers.
*One tactic that is sometimes used is to just grab the ball and head straight for the goal, although this works better when using high health Brawlers such as Bull, [[Ollie]], [[Buster]], [[Hank]], Jacky, Bibi, [[Ash]], Frank, [[Pam]], [[Rosa]], El Primo, [[Darryl]], [[Sam]], [[Buzz]], [[8-Bit]], [[Doug]], and [[Draco]].
*[[Colt]] and [[Brock]] can use their Silver Bullet and Rocket Fuel Gadgets to break down the central walls. This will prevent -tanks and throwers from thriving.
*[[Barley]] is a very good mid/short lane Brawler on this map. He can get mid control, hide behind walls and get kills, and then defend against aggro Brawlers from behind his wall well, while tearing down the enemy defenders.
*[[Sprout]] is also very good, once you score a goal, you can trap the ball, and put it in the corner and use Sprout to block it.
*[[Mortis]], [[Edgar]], [[Shade]], [[Leon]], and [[Kenji]] are viable in this map as many throwers like [[Dynamike]], Barley, [[Larry & Lawrie]], [[Willow]], and Sprout is played in this map, the five will be able to counter these throwers easily.

==Tips==
*Heavyweights and medium-ranged Brawlers should plan on going through the side lanes where there is more walls and bushes for defense.
**[[Nita]], [[Carl]], [[Tara]], and [[Frank]] can force enemies from the center bushes by using the small vertical walls when pressuring them.
*[[Sandy]] can turn the center of this map into a safe haven for heavyweights with the use of his Super. Along with other medium-ranged Brawlers, they should head into the side lanes to charge their Super.
*Sharpshooters and throwers are not recommended on this map because they can be easily pressured by tanks using the 4x4 walls in either lanes. The openness of this map is tempting, but very rarely will much action take place here which they could use.
*High-spread Brawlers will generally be the ones forcing Brawlers from moving towards the center of this map. [[Pam]], [[Emz]], Frank, and [[Poco]] are all very good in handling open areas. 
**Brawlers with longer range can serve to counter enemies attempting to use this strategy. This includes [[Mr. P]] with his Handle with Care Star Power and [[Bea]].

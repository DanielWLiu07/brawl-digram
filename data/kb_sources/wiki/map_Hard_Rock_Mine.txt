==Tips==
*Tanks and other short-ranged Brawlers should sneak in from the side to stop enemy Brawlers from heading directly into action. They should ideally use the left lane as the right side of the bushes is blocked off from the main strips of bushes.
*Control-based Brawler are great on this map as you can push the opponents back to their spawn for the whole game with the horizontal strip of bushes on their side.
*Brawlers like [[Barley]], [[Dynamike]], and other lobbers can stand in the right hand side three tiles worth of grass and shelter behind the sideways L shape of wall. This lets them throw their attacks over the wall into the center and around it. The only way to take damage in this position is if the enemy team has a lobber or if someone creeps down the bushes on the side.
*Using her Grow Light Gadget, [[Rosa]] can connect the bushes on the left and right to create a ring of bushes around the middle. This can allow close-ranged Brawlers such as [[Bull]], [[Darryl]], [[Shelly]], [[Mortis]], [[Bibi]], [[Jacky]], or [[El Primo]] easier passage to the enemy side, or it can allow her to continuously dominate the borders while constantly healing when paired with her Plant Life Star Power. Although, be wary because your opponents could use it to get to your side unnoticed.
*Marksmen and artillery are very weak and vulnerable on this map to tanks. Use medium-ranged Brawlers instead.
*Brawlers with a considerable amount of spread are also great for keeping the center in check as well.
*You can use the right lane to charge your Super and leave once it's charged. Unless you go around the 2x3 wall, you won't be able to do much more than this. Brawlers that may need to do this to charge their Super consist of medium-ranged Brawlers including [[Mr. P]], [[Frank]], [[Spike]], [[Surge]], [[Cordelius]], [[Gale]], and [[Sandy]].
*[[Rico]] is an excellent lane Brawler on this map due to the number of wall segments on either side, allowing for multiple bounces, especially with use of his Super Bouncy Star Power.
*[[8-Bit]] and/or [[Pam]] are great for this map because they can place their turrets behind the small groups of walls to provide prolonged support to teammates. Additionally, Pam's Mama's Squeeze Star Power can be used to deal 800 damage to a group of opponents. However, beware of wall breakers or artilleries.
*[[Sprout]] also does very well on this map because it can block the major chokepoints, which can be game changing. The bushes on each team's side of the map support its Photosynthesis Star Power very well.
*[[Ruffs]] can bounce his attacks off the wall clusters in a similar manner to Rico. With his Air Superiority Star Power, Ruffs can use his Super to clear the bush cluster closer to the enemy spawn so there is less room for the enemy team to move around without revealing themselves.

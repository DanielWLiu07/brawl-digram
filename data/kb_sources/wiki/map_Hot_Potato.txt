==Tips==
*[[Jessie]] can place her turret in the very center of the map, between the two large walls. It can then attack anyone who comes into the center bushes, and keep them distracted, while she sneaks to the enemy base and deals as much damage as she can. She can also use her 2nd Star Power with her second Gadget (Recoil Spring) to deal damage to the safe and nearby enemies.
*Tanks such as [[Bull]], [[El Primo]], [[Rosa]], and [[Darryl]] can camp in the bushes just behind the center bush and not be noticed. Here they can ambush people that are walking by and once Bull, El Primo, and Darryl have their Supers charged they can use it on the enemy's safe and do massive damage.
*When playing with long-ranged [[Brawlers]] like [[Colt]], [[Rico]], [[Piper]], [[Bea]], [[Nani]], [[Janet]], or [[Brock]], it is best to go to the sides of the map where they can snipe. When playing with Rico and equipped with his Super Bouncy Star Power, then hold up behind the T-shaped wall and ricochet off walls.
*[[8-Bit]] being a damage dealer can be played both as a tank and a marksman on this map; however, it's better for them to enter the center lane where they can bush-check. He can also deploy his Super behind any wall so that he and allies can receive a constant attack damage boost.
*Throwers such as [[Barley]], [[Dynamike]], [[Sprout]], [[Grom]], or [[Berry]] should play in the right lane where there are little entrances for them to be taken down as well as having access to damaging the safe.
*Colt and Brock are very strong on this map and can be played in the center or on a lane. With their Speedloader Gadget and Rocket No. Four Star Power respectively, they can control the lane or mid and deal massive damage to the safe.
*[[Nita]] is very good on this map as her attack can pierce through enemies, and it's fairly wide but medium-ranged. She can detect enemies and deal damage to grouped-up enemies in the middle, while her Super combined with her Star Power Hyper Bear is able to deal incredible damage to the safe that racks up quickly. This makes her a good pick in almost any map in Heist except for very open maps that favours long-ranged Brawlers. Otherwise, she will almost guarantee a win with her bear and Star Power.
*If there are any throwers in the opposite team, destroying the L-shaped wall in front of the safe is often a good idea, as it prevents them from taking cover behind the wall while attacking the safe and running around it to avoid damage from lone Brawlers. [[Colt]]'s Silver Bullet Gadget, [[Brock]]'s Rocket Fuel Gadget, [[Griff]]'s Piggy Bank Gadget, and [[Gray]]'s Grand Piano Gadget are particularly good at destroying the wall right at the beginning of the match.
*The [[Darryl]], [[Bull]], [[Edgar]] and [[Kit]] comp is viable in this map as the map has a straight road to reach the safe making it easier to reach the safe. Even if Darryl/Bull do not have their Super, Kit can jump on Darryl/Bull and use his Yarn Balls attack to damage the safe.
*[[Chuck]] can install his Posts in one row easily as there is a straight path towards the enemy safe. He can also damage any enemies trying to attack his team's safe by using the row with his Super.

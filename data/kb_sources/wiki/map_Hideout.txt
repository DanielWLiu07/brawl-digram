==Tips==
*Throwers like Barley, [[Larry & Lawrie]], Dynamike, [[Juju]], and [[Berry]] can use the walls to handle enemies hiding in the nearby bushes. [[Sprout]] might have an advantage in this map due to its unique projectiles provided that the rest of the team can force the enemy into the walls. However, do consider that because of the walls, Sprout's projectiles will bounce, so crucial aim is necessary. [[Tick]] and [[Grom]] may be a viable alternative.
*[[Penny]] and her mortar can be great for enemies who are standing still waiting for an opportunity. Bushes should be destroyed whenever possible, where her Pocket Detonator Gadget can help. [[Mr. P]] can also place his turret behind walls so that the turret is protected from damage.
*The right lane of the map can be used by sharpshooters and other long-ranged Brawlers to pressure enemies hiding near the large wall.
*The left lane is more fit for short to medium-ranged Brawlers. Short-ranged Brawlers have to gamble with the fact that they'll lose their protection once the cacti are destroyed.
**If your team is not negatively impacted from the loss of the cacti, take priority in destroying them.
*An excellent Brawler is [[Spike]] as his attack can spread out spikes and check the bushes. With his Curveball Star Power, the spikes provide more area denial. Note that having a Spike in your team means that you should also have a Brawler to supplement Spike, such as [[Dynamike]], Barley, Larry & Lawrie, or Tick, because Spike's low health makes him vulnerable.
*[[Barley]] is also a great choice because his attack, Super, and Sticky Syrup Mixer Gadget can all keep enemies away. If possible, Barley should be kept behind cover at all times.
*Long-ranged Brawlers such as [[Belle]], [[Mandy]], [[Bea]], [[Nani]], [[Rico]], [[Brock]], [[Maisie]], [[Colt]], [[Angelo]], or [[Piper]], can take control on the side lines, since they are mostly open.

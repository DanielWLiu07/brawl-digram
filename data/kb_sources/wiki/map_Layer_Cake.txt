==Tips==
*Since Layer Cake has barely anything blocking projectiles that go through bushes, [[Piper]], [[Colt]], [[Bea]], [[8-Bit]], [[Rico]], [[Mandy]], and [[Brock]] could be used for this map. With Piper, use her Ambush Star Power to use the bushes effectively.
*Destroying the clumps of walls on the enemy teams side can prove useful later on in the match when they try to fall back to regenerate.
*Sneaking into the clumps of bushes on the enemies side without them noticing provides for an easy defeat.
*[[Jessie]] and [[Belle]] can control the center as their shots hit multiple enemies due to the layer divisions.
*Try pushing the enemy to their last layer to see their movements and pick them off individually.
*Throwers are great if the walls are not destroyed as they can land their shots consistently between the layers. They can also use their main attacks to shower down projectiles and block off the choke points on both sides of the map, thereby limiting and even pushing back enemy movement and allowing allies to gain control of the center of the map. Area control and positioning are key for succeeding in this map.
**[[Mortis]], [[Leon]], [[Buzz]], and [[Crow]] act as okay counters against these Brawlers. Generally, these Brawlers will run away when within sight of them, unless the throwers can defend themselves quite well.
*[[Penny]]'s cannon is good in this map because of the large amount of control it is able to maintain. If Penny places her cannon behind either of the clump of walls near the center, it will be difficult for the enemies to destroy it.
*[[Mr. P]] can be very useful in this map because he can attack enemies hiding behind walls with his main attack. The same can be said about his Super. If placed correctly, it can give the enemy team a lot of trouble (especially with the "Revolving Door" Star Power).
*[[Surge]], [[Edgar]], Crow, [[Kit]], or [[El Primo]] can be a useful offensive Brawler in this map. Their Supers allows them to jump over obstacles, easily eliminating enemies and recharging their Supers.
*Try not to get pushed back into your spawn area because then it will be easy for the enemy team to gain control of the center and spawn trap your team.

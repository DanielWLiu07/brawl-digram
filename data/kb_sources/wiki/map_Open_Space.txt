== Tips ==

* Marksmen, such as [[Brock]], [[Piper]], [[Maisie]], [[Janet]], [[Angelo]], and [[Belle]] are solid options, as the map is mainly open.
* [[Gene]] is a solid gem carrier on this map. His split, long-ranged main attack can allow him to chip enemies from a distance, preventing them from healing and pushing them back. It also charges his game-changing Super pretty easily due to its width.
*Artilleries and Tanks will struggle on this map as the map is very open and there are barely any walls to hide behind.

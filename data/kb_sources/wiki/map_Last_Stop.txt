==Tips==
* Brawlers that excel at both long and short range are great on this map. For example, [[Shelly]] and [[Nani]]. Shelly can easily defeat Brawlers that are attempting a sneak attack in the bushes, and her Clay Pigeons Gadget can allow her to force sharpshooters in the middle to retreat. 
* Marksmen and other long-ranged Brawlers have no cover to use in the middle, so they should stick to the bush just below the gem mine. Although more risky, they can also travel to the top of the L-shaped bush to apply pressure to enemies that have just respawned.
* [[Tara]], [[Leon]], [[Buzz]], [[Spike]], and [[El Primo]] are great Brawlers for this map as they can sneak into one of the bushes and ambush enemies with their deadly Supers to steal the gems from the enemies.

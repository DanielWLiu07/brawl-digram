==Tips==
*[[Rosa]] is viable here with her Unfriendly Bushes Gadget and her Plant Life Star Power as her Gadget slows down enemies as they are always in the bushes and her Star Power helping because she sometimes has to go to the enemy side's bushes that makes her heal thanks to her Plant Life and help her take more fire.
*Sharpshooters are decent-good Brawlers as the lanes are very narrow and makes it easier for them to hit their shots. [[Brock]] and [[Colt]] are some great examples, because they can push back enemy Brawlers with their attacks, and also destroy cover like walls and bushes with their Supers and Gadgets.
**[[Piper]] is also good here as her Ambush Star Power helps her attacks to deal more damage when in a bush. Since the lanes are narrow, she can hit her shots easily and deal massive damage if done right.
**[[Mandy]] also has longer range when focused in the bushes, making her a good pick to outrange sharpshooters with her In My Sights Star Power. Her Super can be used to wipe out anyone hiding in the main clump of bushes.
*Brawlers like [[Griff]], [[Bo]], [[Lola]], [[Max]], [[Pearl]], [[Chester]], and [[Tara]] are very effective here because of a slight arc in their attacks, making it harder to dodge their shots. They can also be used to counter close-ranged assassins.
*Another great option for this map would be [[Bea]], as her role as a Marksman. She can deal high damage if you can land her first and second shot together. Her Super covers a large area and can slow down opponents, making it easier for your teammates to eliminate them.
*[[Edgar]] is also a really good Brawler in this map because of his ability to jump over the multitude of walls on the map and ambush multiple opponents, possibly team wiping. Using his Hardcore Gadget or his Fisticuffs Star Power boosts his effectiveness and provides more survivability.
*Throwers like [[Tick]], [[Grom]], [[Dynamike]], and [[Barley]] can attack over the walls in the middle, preventing enemies from taking certain paths while hiding behind walls safely. With their respective Gadgets and Star Powers (Mine Mania for Tick, Radio Check for Grom, Demolition for Dynamike, and Extra Noxious for Barley) to boost damage, they can provide excellent area control at the end of the bush clump.
*Aggressive brawlers with dashing abilities are very useful in controlling the map. [[Mortis]], [[Kaze]], [[Kenji]], and [[Mina]] can move to the middle of the map quickly and stay behind iron walls, frightening off enemy throwers or snipers, so they can't quickly attack your teammates in the top part of the middle grass. [[Buzz]], [[Cordelius]], and [[Lily]] will have similar effects as they charge their super when enemies are around. Brawlers good at hiding such as [[Leon]], [[Lily]], and [[Kit]] can flexibly switch places of ambush and look for chances of attack.

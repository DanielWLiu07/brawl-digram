==Tips ==
*Treat this as a Brawl Ball version of [[Feast or Famine]]. You will be rewarded for going to the center, but you are more likely to be ambushed. 
*The [[Gears|Speed Gear]] is very useful in this map because there are a lot of bushes.
*[[Rosa]] is very strong on this map, especially if she has her Plant Life Star Power equipped because there are a lot of bushes. Use her Unfriendly Bushes Gadget to slow down enemies in bushes, or her Grow Light Gadget to connect the bushes on the left and/or right. 
*Another option is to use high-spread Brawlers such as [[Pam]], Poco, Frank, [[Sandy]], and [[Emz]] that can block off certain sections of the bush border with their main attacks. [[Poco]] is also a viable option, as he can supplement the health of his teammates and can use his wide attack radius to scan for concealed enemy Brawlers hiding in bushes.
*Sharpshooters are risky to play on this map because of how much space the bush border takes, make sure you're playing long-ranged Brawlers that can reliably burst otherwise you'll have to play at the side lanes.
*If you're playing at the side lanes, do not use ambushing strategies as the bush strips can be checked single-handedly by most sharpshooters.
*[[Frank]] is a good choice as his Super can clear enemies hiding behind the 1x4 walls in the middle. Along with his high health, his wall-clearing Super has lots of range, so unless the enemy team has a wall-breaker, Frank can basically destroy all possible barriers that protect the enemy goal.
*High-health Brawlers could kick the ball into the goal because there are less enemies in a side of the map. 
*On this map, it's good to have wall breakers like [[Colt]], [[Shelly]], [[Dynamike]], Frank, [[El Primo]], [[Piper]], Grom, [[Nani]], [[Pearl]], or [[Brock]]. Breaking down walls will give a disadvantage to tanks and make it easier to score.
*[[Tara]] is a good choice because her Psychic Enhancer Gadget can let her see any enemies hiding in the center grass. 
*[[Rico]] is a strong pick on this map because he can bounce his shots on the vertical 4x1 to hit the enemies hiding behind their cluster of walls.
*[[Grom]] is an extremely good pick in this map, you can see all the enemies in the bushes with his Watchtower Gadget. This keeps the enemies behind the wall, so your team can easily kill them with Brawlers such as [[Penny]], Frank, [[Rosa]], El Primo, Emz, [[Jacky]], [[Buzz]], or other Brawlers with a high damage output or a piercing attack. Make sure to put the Gadget behind a wall. Both Star Powers are good on this map.
**The same with [[Janet]]'s Drop The Bass Gadget, you can see enemies in bushes and they can't heal at the same time. With her Stage View Star Power you can also see enemies in bushes while damaging them.
*Buzz can use his X-Ray-Shades Gadget to let him and his teammates see enemies hiding in the center grass.

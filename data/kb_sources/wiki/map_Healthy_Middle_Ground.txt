==Tips==
*Teleports can be used to surprise or distract, depending on the Brawler of each match. It is recommended not to use them immediately as the enemy team may still be positioning and make an immediate attack that can easily eliminate you. However, there are several exceptions: (1) You are a skilled assassin equipped with super, gadget, or even hypercharge, and your enemies have no measures of countering you. (2) Your team is a combo with [[Buster]], and he needs to have his supercharge. 
*Marksman and Damage Dealer Brawlers are good choices, whether to check the bushes or quickly teleport to finish off a weakened opponent. For marksman, [[Brock]] is a optimal choice for the map, for his gadget and super can efficiently destroy enemy bushes and walls, creating a safe and suitable output environment for his team. [[Mandy]] is also very useful in the map due to the shape of the bushes, as her super can wipe out enemies who try to hide inside the bushes or behind the walls. [[Piper]] with the Ambush star power can also deal considerable damage while ensuring her own safety, and her super can change landscapes as well. 
*Control brawlers such as [[Gray]] and [[Gene]] are also good for the map. [[Gray]]'s gadget can pull enemies through him and his team, securing a quick takedown if the chance is seized. He can also place supers among the bushes, confusing his enemies about the true location of him and his teammates. [[Gene]]'s main attack is very useful for detecting the enemies who try to advance through the bushes, and his super's pull also secures a kill if used properly. 
*Certain tank combos are also good choices for the map. [[Buster]] is a good choice, as the map is covered in bushes, and it is difficult for his enemies to detect him and his teammates' locations. [[Buster]] can wait until his teammates charge his super and hypercharge, so they can move out and push their enemies backwards. [[Hank]] is also a good choice, as he can stay behind the wall and push back enemies using his large attack area.

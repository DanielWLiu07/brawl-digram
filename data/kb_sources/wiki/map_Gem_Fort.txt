==Tips==
*[[Gale]] can prevent enemies from the entering the center of the map by using his Super. His Spring Ejector Gadget in order to launch teammates to the center early on in the game to gain immediate control.
*[[Jacky]] should play outside of these walls if the zone is controlled checking the bushes for teammates and forcing enemy Brawlers to move into the center where teammates waiting near walls can finish them off.
**Brawlers that can handle the enemies after Jacky pressures them into the center include [[Sandy]], [[Poco]], [[Carl]], [[Buzz]], [[Penny]], [[Mr. P]], and [[Frank]] which are all capable of maintaining control of the center for their teammates.
*[[Rosa]] should connect the bushes with her Grow Light Gadget which she can then ambush enemies easily. This will give her teammates more space to move unobserved by enemies and in order to launch sudden attacks on retreating enemies. Her Unfriendly Bushes Gadget can also prove equally useful  because you can see where enemies are hiding and damage them.
*[[Jessie]], [[Nita]], [[Barley]], [[Penny]], [[Amber]], [[Gale]], and [[Emz]] should force enemies out of the center using a combination of their Super, Gadget, and main attack. All these Brawlers have decent damage output and excellent area denial, so they are ideal for preventing enemies from entering the center of the map. Jessie's turret Scrappy should be placed in the top section of the fort away from openings.
**Sweeping Brawlers such as [[Bo]], [[Lola]], [[Chester]], [[Pearl]], [[Pam]], [[Gene]], [[Shelly]], or [[Tara]] can deal with opponents camping in the center even without breaking any walls. Bo should use his Snare a Bear Star Power to gain more time for attacks after enemies are stunned.
*Be aware that enemy Brawlers may be hiding in bushes on the extremes of the map, in order to launch a surprise attack or to hide. Enemy Gem carriers may be hiding in the bushes waiting until their teammates take out Brawlers. Abilities like Tara's Psychic Enhancer Gadget, Bo's Circling Eagle Star Power, Grom's Watchtower Gadget or Buzz's X-Ray-Shades Gadget will be very useful for spotting opponents in the bushes.
*After gaining control of the center of the map, the top two corners are ideal places to hide if there are no opponent throwers. There are bushes to confuse enemies and the corner is large enough to dodge diagonal attacks.
*When retreating, head through the center lane so that the walls can protect you from enemy fire. This should make it slightly harder for enemies to ambush you.
*[[Sprout]]’s Super can be used to block of some of the entrances to the center of the map, making it harder for the enemy team to access any available Gems and prevent enemies from launching attacks on allies.
*[[Mr. P]]'s porters can help scan bushes and he can attack enemies hiding behind walls waiting for an ambush. He can use his Porter Reinforcements Gadget to reveal the locations of concealed enemies.
*[[Colt]] can use his Silver bullet Gadget to destroy the walls at center in the enemy side or destroy a part of the bushes on each side of the map to turn the game in favor of your team.
*[[Sandy]] is a decent pick because of his Super. If you can get control over the center and get his Super, you and your teammates can sneak from one of the lanes to one of the bushes in the center ring or move from one of the center bushes to another while reducing the success of an enemy ambush.
*A good strategy for this map is to use [[Amber]] with two other long-ranged Brawlers. Her continuous attack is excellent for checking bushes and she can burn all the bushes on the map with her Super and her Fire Starters Gadget (better aided if coupled with her Wild Flames Star Power).
*[[Carl]]'s wide projectile width can help him to easily check the bushes on the side for any enemies. The walls also help the pickaxe return faster.

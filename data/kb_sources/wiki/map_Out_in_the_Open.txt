==Tips==
#Sharpshooters, such as [[Piper]], [[Bonnie]], [[Bea]], [[Brock]], [[Belle]], and [[Mandy]], work essentially well here. With [[Piper]]'s Ambush Star Power, she can also use the bushes at the center to utilize the damage. [[Brock]] is a top choice of the map, as he can destroy the walls near the river, enabling his team to attack from 3 different directions towards the enemies. The explosion radius of his main attack is also very good at detecting enemies in the bushes. [[Mandy]] is also strong in this map as there are many chokepoints and narrow passages where enemies will probably walk through. She can use her super to deal loads of damage in these situations. [[Gene]] is also a very good brawler in the map, as his main attack detects the enemies in the bushes efficiently and can drive them out. 
#[[Eve]], [[Juju]], and [[Angelo]] can use the water on the left of the wall to get a good angle on enemies and quickly retreat when getting attacked. One useful tip for [[Eve]] in the map is that she can put her super on the spring pad. When it flies across the map and lands at the enemy side, her super hatches at the same time. This means that enemies have no chance of destroying her super and will have to waste ammo dealing with her babies.
#Assassins (especially those with invisibilities, such as [[Leon]], [[Kit]], [[Kaze]], and [[Lily]]) are suitable for the map. They can sneak among the bushes and surprise-attack the enemies.

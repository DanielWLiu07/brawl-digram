==Tips==
*Sharpshooters, such as [[Mandy]], [[Colt]], [[8-Bit]], [[Bea]], [[Piper]], and [[Brock]], Etc., are decent choices in this map due to the open space. Sharpshooters should still have control-based abilities and/or wall-breaking abilities if they would like to be more viable.
*Heavyweights and assassins such as should stick to the walls on the sides. They will have more protection if they use the left lane to hide behind the barrels.
*When attempting to make goals, sharpshooters should stay near teammates that can handle short-ranged combat.
*[[Jacky]] can make a quick dash for the ball in beginning of games with use of her Pneumatic Booster Gadget. In higher [[Trophies|Trophy]] matches, this strategy is better countered with Brawlers that outrange her. Instead, Jacky should use her Gadget Pneumatic Booster to put immediate pressure onto the enemy team by curbing the side lane barriers facing the center.
*[[Sandy]] can use his Super to turn the center of the map into protection. He should charge his Super on the side lanes before heading into the center of the map. It is best to place his Super down so it adequately touches at least 4 bushes.
*[[Lou]] is good on this map because his Super provides some area denial, and his long range is well suited to this map.
*The center of the map is both open and confined. You can trap enemy Brawlers here with the use of Brawlers with high spread such as [[Emz]], [[Poco]], [[Frank]], [[Gene]], and [[Pam]].

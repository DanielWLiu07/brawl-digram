==Tips==
*Especially in this map, the beginning of the game is crucial. Do whatever needed to defeat enemies and gain control of the two central bushes. The team who has control of the center will be almost unstoppable after doing so. Heavyweights of the map will have the bushes to ambush enemies. Sharpshooters can use the open area an enemy needs to pass to get to the bushes, and kill approaching enemies quickly. Mid ranged Brawlers and throwers can use the walls to attack enemies safely. In short, the team with control of the middle will be very hard to defeat. Another reason contributing to this is the very narrow spaces to move past the lakes and out of the spawn areas, which does not allow players to dodge shots easily and get to their enemies.
*This map is good for heavyweights such as [[Bull]], [[Rosa]], [[Bibi]], [[Draco]], Darryl, [[Frank]], and [[El Primo]]. [[Darryl]] and Bull can use their Supers to roll/charge along the water. Tanks should focus on maintaining control of the sides from enemy Brawlers.
**If you play Darryl, Bull, or any Brawler who can charge or dive with their Super, just know that their Super can't be used to travel completely through the water from the center to the back.
*[[Emz]] is great for this map. Her wide ranged attack can clear bushes quickly and her Super can be used to cover an even greater amount of bushes to locate camping Brawlers.
*[[Piper]], [[Nani]], and [[Tick]] are highly used on this map for their range and damage outputs. Three of these Brawlers are capable of destroying obstacles, but they should only destroy obstacles that are frequently used as cover by enemies such as the walls on the back of this map.
**Other options include [[Brock]], [[Colt]], and [[Barley]] with Brock and Colt able to control openings with use of their Incendiary Star Power and Speedloader Gadget respectively. Both Tick and Barley should use the walls on the back of this map, only for entering bushes directly, unless they are protected by a heavyweight.
* An effective strategy with Tick on this map is to throw mines into the bushes as soon as you are within their range, and basically make the bush unsafe for enemies to enter. This will discourage them, especially close-ranged ones, from going into the bushes and help your teammates gain control of the bushes, which, as mentioned above, is extremely important in this map. Once enemies are killed and the team has control of the bushes, Tick can throw mines on the narrow paths of this map as the enemies are spawning, so that in an attempt to push your team out of the center, they'll have to move on the mines and take heavy damage. All of this goes on to justify Tick's immense potential as a control Brawler, hence making him useful on this map.
*[[Mr. P]] can scout Brawlers with his porters and use their main attack bounce to damage enemies from all sides if they attempt to enter their bush. Piper, [[Bea]], [[Belle]], and Brock will have a much harder time in taking out his porters as well.
*[[Rosa]] can use her Grow Light Gadget to connect both the bush centers. Due to her close range, she'll need to ambush enemies using bushes and walls, and connecting both bush centers will facilitate this very well. 
*[[Bo]], [[Grom]], and [[Tara]] are useful because of their first Star Power and Gadgets respectively as they can be used to help teammates detect enemies in the important central bushes, protecting against ambushes from closer-ranged Brawlers. Tara's Support From Beyond Gadget can also be used to detect enemies in bushes and simultaneously give an offensive counterattack to the enemy team.
*The openings of this map are considerably small and once blocked off by [[Sprout]]'s Super, opponents will have to take very time-consuming and unsafe detours to get to their desired location.
*Tara's Psychic Enhancer Gadget, Grom's Watchtower Gadget, and Bo's Circling Eagle Star Power can be useful for scouting and finding foes in bushes.
*On Canal Grande, try to destroy the walls and bushes in the the center as a low health Brawler. This will make it harder for tanks and throwers to thrive. Piper, Colt, Nani, and Brock can easily do this with their Supers.
*In the middle of the map on either side, there is a three tile wall. Sprout's Super can be placed directly on the inner side of the wall to create a perfect cage. It can be used as a safeguard or shield, or a cage to trap other non-thrower enemies.

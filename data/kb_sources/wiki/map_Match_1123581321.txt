==Tips==

* [[Melodie]] is a strong Brawler for this map, given that her Super has 3 dashes that allow her to quickly gain control of the ball, and score goals.
* Alternatively, you could also use [[Darryl]], [[Edgar]], or [[Kit]] as they can shoot the ball and use their auto-charging Supers to self-pass the ball and score.
* The map is quite open so using long-ranged Brawlers like [[Brock]], [[Bea]], [[Piper]], [[Colt]], [[Nani]], and [[Belle]] are an ideal choice.
* Artillery Brawlers are not recommended as there are not many walls for them to hide behind and defeat opponents.
* Brawlers that have wall-breaking abilities like Brock, Colt, Piper, [[Tara]] and [[Stu]] with his Breakthrough Gadget would be ideal for this map as they can break the walls to allow your team to score easier.

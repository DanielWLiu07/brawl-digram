==Tips==
*As one should normally do in Gem Grab, the Gem carrier will stay in the middle to collect the Gems, while the support Brawlers either stay with the Gem carrier or split off to the left and right sides of the middle section. Ideally, each Brawler would take one side to defend or push back the enemy team from progressing forward or at least from the Gem carrier.
*Always try to check the bushes for enemy aggressive short-range Brawlers, especially when the countdown starts.
*Use the middle walls when taking cover, but be aware of any Brawlers in the bush heavy sections.
*When the countdown begins, the ideal spot to retreat is the bushes behind the walls on your side.
*Great supports include [[Pam]], [[Sandy]], [[Byron]], and [[Poco]]. Sandy may need to go through the side lanes to charge his Super.
*Medium-ranged Brawlers such as [[Mr. P]], [[Emz]], Spike, [[Surge]], Poco, and [[Tara]] work extremely well. You may need to use them to counter Brawlers with high spread.
*[[Brock]] can control 5 bushes on the enemy side and can destroy the side bushes with his Super or Gadget which most of his counters rely on.
*Using [[Carl]] as a lane Brawler in this map offers very good value as he has one of the widest attacks in the game and he doesn't have the most range, so spam your attack and take the enemy lane Brawler down (if your enemy lane is a [[Colette]], [[Amber]], or [[Spike]], switch lanes immediately as they are some of Carl's biggest counters).
*[[Rico]] can go to the left lane and he becomes a big threat to the enemy team as he can camp in that lane's bushes. He becomes into a bigger threat with his Super Bouncy Star Power because he can deal more damage. Once Rico has his Super charged, he can easily get his Super again, making him a very good choice and constantly getting enemies to low health, or even defeating them.
*[[Rosa]] can do very well on this map especially with her Plant Life Star Power and either Gadget. Other tanks like [[Bull]], [[Darryl]], and [[El Primo]] are also good. However, at high Trophy matches, players will be checking the bushes constantly, so tanks may not be viable.
*Another good Brawler is [[Leon]], who can use his mobility and Super to ambush enemies with his massive damage at point-blank range. His Lollipop Drop Gadget can be placed behind the walls near the center.

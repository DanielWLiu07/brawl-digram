==Tips==
*Due to this map's large amount of bushes, tank Brawlers with high damage potential are extremely useful.
*Artillery can get pressured easily, but its high-risk, high-reward scenario can amount in massive area damage and control.
*Enemies can make use of the bushes on your side as well, so Brawlers who can destroy terrain can make it harder for enemy Brawlers to flank.
*Destroying terrain on this map also makes it harder to use [[El Primo]], [[Shelly]], [[Pearl]], [[Frank]], or [[Bull]].
*Use the bushes to your best advantage and pressure enemies through flanking from more than one side.
**Brawlers that can flank quite well include [[Rosa]], [[Jacky]], [[Buster]], and [[Bibi]]. Rosa can connect bushes with her Grow Light Gadget and her Plant Life Star Power makes her very hard to take out. Brawlers that flank in the center top of this map should have medium to long range.
*The center of this should be used by high spread Brawlers such as [[Poco]], [[Sandy]], [[Frank]], [[Penny]], [[Pam]], or [[Emz]] which can slow down enemies from collecting Gems while your teammates pressure them on both sides.
*[[Tara]] is one of the best Brawlers on this map, due to her Psychic Enhancer Gadget that can reveal enemy positions. She can also hide and wait for the right moment to Super and wipe the enemy team.
*[[Colette]] is an amazing pick on this map, due to her ability move up the left lane and consistently poke enemy Brawlers hiding behind walls, making it harder for the enemy team to push up.
*[[Mortis]] is a strong pick on this map, due to his ability to easily enter the enemy side of the base and defeat the entire enemy team. His Survival Shovel Gadget and Creepy Harvest Star Power are particularly useful if one aims for this feat, as it allows Mortis to recharge faster and continuously heal to avoid being defeated before completing his team wipe.
*It often matters from which choke-point you will travel through to defeat the enemy. If you are attacking from the center, wait for the enemy to collect and then attack. If you are attacking from the left side, try to attack enemies who are hiding behind the walls, like throwers. Usually, Brawlers come from the center, so this might work in some cases.
*[[Squeak]] can be used offensively in this map to find Brawlers (especially marksmen like [[Piper]], [[Bea]], [[Angelo]], [[Mandy]], [[Bonnie]], or [[Brock]]) in bushes as his attacks stick to the enemies or walls.
*[[Belle]] can place her Nest Egg Gadget on the ends of the spiral bushes to trap enemies. Her bouncing attack can also detect enemies in bushes if they are very close together.
*[[Amber]] is also good on this map because she can continuously sweep bushes with her attack. She can also burn down bushes with her Super on the enemies' side to force the enemy from hiding.
*[[Bo]] can place his traps in the gap between the spiraling bush clusters to deter enemies from entering your side. He can also place his mines on the Gem Mine, made more effective with his Snare a Bear Star Power, to further holding off enemy progress.
*[[Gene]] is a good pick on this map because of the area his attacks cover. In combination with vision gear, the enemies will be visible for a long time after he damages them from afar. His  Super is great for pulling enemies with Gems and eliminating them. His Lamp Blowout Gadget can be used to counter aggro brawlers and assassins.
*[[Jacky]] is good at collecting the Gems at the start of the game. Because she is fast and tanky, she will get to the middle faster than other brawlers, and she can attack enemies behind the unbreakable wall.
*[[Dynamike]] is an excellent choice here, due to his obstacle destroying capabilities, and his massive control. His Satchel Charge Gadget allows him to stun Gem carriers, and his throwing capabilities can get past walls that carriers hide against
*[[Crow]] is also an excellent choice. His poison daggers can chip away at health, revealing enemies hiding in their bushes, reduce healing from enemies, and his Super allows him to catch up to Gem carriers running away.
*[[Rosa]] has two very good Gadgets that can help players with the map. Her Unfriendly Bushes Gadget allows her to reveal and slow enemies if you are unsure where they are in their teams bush, and Growth Light can connect the center with her team's bush, allowing for stealthy collection.

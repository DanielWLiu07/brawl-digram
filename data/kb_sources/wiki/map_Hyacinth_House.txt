==Tips==
*Tanks, such as [[Draco]], [[Bibi]], [[Bull]], [[Darryl]], [[Hank]], [[Frank]], [[Ash]], [[Buster]], [[Rosa]], [[Jacky]] are especially strong when engaging in short-ranged combat.
*Brawlers that can summon pets that follow Brawlers, such as [[Nita]], [[Tick]], [[Larry & Lawrie]], [[Mr. P]], [[Charlie]], [[Eve]], [[Ash]], and [[Tara]] with her Black Portal Star Power can seek out other Brawlers and find out where they are. They can also act as a meat shield against non-piercing attacks.
*Artillery Brawlers like [[Barley]], [[Dynamike]], [[Tick]], [[Grom]],  [[Larry & Lawrie]], [[Sprout]], and [[Juju]] are suitable Brawlers as they can use their lobbed attacks to camp outside the middle bush and hide behind the other walls since going to the middle bush will most likely result in being defeated by a tank. Their disadvantage is that when the map is closing, they will have to go into the center where they can be easily killed by tanks and assassins.
*[[Stu]] can be excellent in this map if you are experienced.  Stu is rather a low health Brawler, having a healing Brawler is a good choice. Stu's Breakthrough Gadget does especially well on this map, due to the large amounts of walls. With this Gadget, he can easily target and kill enemies behind walls.
*[[Sandy]] is a decent choice, but the fact that most of the map has narrow paths his attack can easily hit enemies. His Rude Sands Star Power is useful for revealing enemy brawlers, because with this Star Power, his Super will deal damage, thus, enemies will get spotted.
*[[Rico]] and [[Ruffs]] can be considered decent choices because of all the walls which their attacks can bounce off as they can find enemies in the bushes as well, especially when using Rico's Super Bouncy Star Power. 
*[[Gale]] with his Spring Ejector Gadget can be useful on this map. He can plant his launch pad in the bushes, making it so that enemies who walk into it will be flung out of the bushes. This can also be fatal to you and your duo partner if you've forgotten where you placed it. If Gale needs more time for this setup, he can use his Blustery Blow Star Power in conjunction with the launch pads. He can also use his Gadget to make enemies hesitant to cross the launch pads and trap them in the poison clouds for a few seconds.
*[[Tara]]'s Psychic Enhancer Gadget can be used to locate enemy Brawlers camping in the vast amount of bushes. In addition, her Black Portal Star Power spawns a shade that can seek out enemies within the bushy terrain of the center of the map, potentially flagging up the whereabouts of hidden enemies if they are present.
*[[Bo]]'s Circling Eagle Star Power also helps well, as he gets to detect enemies in bushes of a certain radius before they see him  for the entire duration of the match.
*[[Edgar]] may also be good with his Let's Fly Gadget. He can hunt down enemies easily with the pretty large jump distance he has in his Super, which actually helps him in every game mode. Plus, Edgar reloads ammo really quick so he can swipe out brawlers easily. The best team with Edgar is [[Poco]], as Poco can heal Edgar while he is fighting other Brawlers and healing himself.
*When playing [[Leon]], you can use his Clone Projector gadget to find enemy Brawlers, or you can use his Lollipop Drop gadget to become invisible will moving into bushes, making yourself undetectable to any unsuspecting enemies.
*[[Grom]] is really strong on this map due to his attack and his Watchtower Gadget. You can either camp in the middle of the map to control play charging his Super, and place his Watchtower Gadget behind a wall so it takes less damage and lasts longer. Additionally, his Foot Patrol Star Power is useful for escaping from other ambushers and tanks.
*Artillery brawlers are recommended on this map and you can also use Brawlers that can break through walls like [[Colt]], [[El Primo]], [[Piper]], [[Tick]], [[Dynamike]], [[Brock]], [[Pearl]], [[Frank]], [[Nani]], [[Bull]], [[Shelly]], or [[Grom]].
*[[Janet]] with her Super would dominate the middle, knowing where her enemies are. When playing Duo Showdown, she could use her super to help her teammate take over the bushy area. Her Drop the Bass gadget would also provide value because of the big area damage and if not facing artillery, the turret could stay there for 20 seconds (per one use). Note that this strategy has not been tested in a real match yet.
*Since Brawlers like [[Shelly]], [[Bull]], [[Darryl]], [[Spike]], [[Leon]], [[Griff]], [[El Primo]], [[Edgar]], [[Fang]], and [[Buzz]] deal massive amounts of damage at a closer range, they have a high advantage in this map due to the number.
